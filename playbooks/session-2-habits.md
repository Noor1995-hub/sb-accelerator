# Session 2 — habits

delaware-side material; never ships. A **1-hour session**, one week
after Session 1, with the vault owner. Session 1 filled the
vault; this session is about whether they have started actually
using it, and it is the only planned touchpoint before hypercare properly
starts.

## The three moves

The vault only pays off if these three become reflexes. Name all three,
plainly, and ask which ones have actually started happening:

1. **Ask before a meeting.** Open the daily note's prep section for that
   meeting before walking in — the entities, projects and people it names
   are already there, written the evening before. The habit to break is
   reconstructing that context from memory or a scroll through old mail.
2. **Dump after.** Whatever mattered in a meeting — a decision, a fact about
   an organisation, a person worth remembering — goes into the vault the
   same day: a recording synced automatically, a note dropped in
   `10-inbox/`, or a line said straight into `/update-entity` or
   `/log-decision`. The habit to break is carrying it in your head until it
   is forgotten or garbled.
3. **Trust the brief.** Once the first two moves are actually happening,
   stop re-deriving what the note already says. The prep section exists so
   the executive does not re-research a relationship the vault already
   tracked.

Where one of the three hasn't taken yet, spend the time there rather than
moving on — a session that covers all three shallowly is less useful than
one that fixes the one that's stuck.

## Read the first week's numbers together

Run `/health-check` live, with the vault owner watching, and read the numbers
out loud — the same content-free counts `hypercare-guide.md` explains how
to interpret. This is the first real evidence of whether the three moves
above are landing: a coverage number that's already thin, or meetings still
arriving with no project linked, says which habit needs the push this
session gives it.

## Tune the brief

If how the vault writes to the executive isn't landing right yet — too
formal, too long, missing the register they actually use — this is the
moment to fix it, not to wait for the next tune-up. Re-run `/setup-my-brain`
in its own re-run mode, but scope it to **`communication-style` only**:
walk straight to that file, show what's there now, and revise it. Do not
re-run the other four files or re-open seeding in this session — the point
is a five-minute correction, not a second Session 1.

## Agree the hypercare cadence

Close by agreeing, out loud, two things that carry the next three weeks:

- **The hypercare cadence** — weekly `/health-check` through weeks 2–4,
  per `hypercare-guide.md`.
- **The vault owner's weekly rhythm** — when in their week they run it, and
  what they do with what it shows (share with delaware on consent, or just
  read it themselves), said out loud now rather than left a private
  assumption.
