## Your day

1. **The vault opens on today's note**, written the evening before: a recap, then each meeting today with who's coming and what happened last time. The calendar icon at the far left brings you back to it.
2. **Waiting for you**, at the top, lists meetings and decisions filed without certainty. Tick **Keep** when a row looks right, **Dismiss** when it is noise.
3. **During the day**, write under **Your notes**, and what you have to do under **Your to do's**. Free text is fine; "Friday" or "14/10" in a to do becomes its deadline.
4. **Need a meeting in now?** Ask for **`/sync-recordings`**: it fetches new meetings, asks you per meeting — accept, dismiss or change — then updates your project pages, with your notes ticked **Include**, and asks about the decisions it found. Mail and Teams, and tomorrow's note, still come in the evening.
5. **Something else to keep?** Say **`/add`** — notes or a transcript you paste, or the conversation you just had with Claude. It asks what it can't tell, shows one line for your OK, and updates your project pages straight away.
6. **Lots to fill in?** Say **`/update-tables`**: you get your People, Projects or Entities table as an Excel with only what you can change; fill it in, add rows, and it is written back for you after a preview.
7. **In the evening** your notes are read, new to do's go to **To Do's**, and tomorrow's note is written, on its own — or now, with **`/evening-sync`**. With mail and Teams switched on, a **Mail and Teams** box adds what needs you.

## The sidebar

One menu; after **Start here** — this page — each entry opens a table. **Daily notes** — earlier days · **Thoughts & ideas** — your own notes; tick **Include** to have one read tonight · **To Do's** — what you have to do, per project, soonest deadline first; tick **Done** once it's done · **Meetings** — every meeting, and notes from email or voice memos · **Projects** — your projects and responsibilities · **People** — who you work with · **Entities** — the companies, teams and other groups you work with · **Decisions** — what was decided · **Input** — what the evening run picks up tonight. **Library** opens the rest: resources, heavy assets, the archive, your private notes and what changed recently.

To ask for anything, say it in your own words: "log that decision", "start a project for…", "write tomorrow's note now", "report this to delaware". The details are in [[How it works]].

> [!info] Nothing leaves this vault unless you say so
> Only `/report-feedback` and `/health-check` talk to delaware — counts and versions, never your notes — and only after your yes on the exact text.
